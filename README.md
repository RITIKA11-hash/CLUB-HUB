# ClubHub - College Societies & Cells Management System

A simple, responsive web application for managing college societies, technical cells, events, auditions, circulars, and student members.

---

## 🏛️ Societies & Cells
1. **C&C** — Career and Counselling Cell
2. **Srijan** — Art & Creativity Club (Painting, Sketching, and Canvas Art)
3. **Tarannum** — Music Club (Vocal, Instrumental, and Band Auditions)
4. **Ananya** — Literary Club (Debate, Poetry, and Creative Writing)
5. **IIC** — Institution's Innovation Council (Student Startups and Innovation)
6. **UCC & DA** — University Computer Cell & Digital Affairs (Technical & Web Initiatives)

---

## ✨ Features
- **Societies & Cells Showcase**: Details on student heads, faculty advisors, active member counts, and cell objectives.
- **Events & Auditions**: Schedule for upcoming college fests, workshops, and audition selection trials.
- **Notice Board**: Categorized official circulars (Urgent, Audition, and General Notices).
- **Members Roster**: Searchable and filterable list of active members across all societies.
- **Event Registration**: Quick registration for students with instant attendee tracking.
- **Data Persistence**: Uses browser LocalStorage so all entries remain saved.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack
- **HTML5**: Page structure and semantic layout.
- **CSS3**: Responsive design, card layouts, and styling.
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)**: Dynamic rendering, search filters, form validation, and LocalStorage management.

---

## 🚀 How to Run
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open **`index.html`** in any web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Brave).
